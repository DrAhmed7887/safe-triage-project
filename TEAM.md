## Team Overview

We are the **Avengers** — a multidisciplinary team from the American University in Cairo (AUC), School of Business. Our capstone thesis project, **SAFE-Triage**, is a research-stage AI-powered triage decision-support system designed specifically for Egyptian hospitals.

The system is currently available as a public research demonstration at [safe-triage-ai.web.app](https://safe-triage-ai.web.app), benchmarked against retrospective datasets, and submitted to the MedGemma Impact Challenge on Kaggle.

## Harvard Application

### Why This Team?

1. **Real Clinical Problem** — 86% ED staff violence rates and 32% preventable mortality in Egyptian hospitals. This isn't theoretical — our physician-developer has seen it firsthand.

2. **Working Research Demonstration** — SAFE-Triage is a working public prototype with reproducible retrospective benchmark metrics. It is not clinically deployed or prospectively validated.

3. **Novel Contribution** — First system to handle Egyptian Arabic dialect for emergency triage, with 1,858 colloquial medical keywords and MedGemma quality assurance.

4. **Safety-First Philosophy** — "AI Extracts → Rules Decide → Humans Confirm" puts patient safety above model accuracy.

5. **Validated Performance** — 35/36 exact ESI agreement (97.2%; 95% Wilson CI 85.8%-99.5%), 36/36 within-one-level agreement (100.0%; 95% Wilson CI 90.4%-100.0%), and 0/36 critical under-triage (95% Wilson CI 0.0%-9.6%) on the MIETIC expert-validated benchmark. See `backend/benchmarks/` and `scripts/wilson_ci.py` for reproducible results.

6. **LMIC Focus** — Built for low- and middle-income country hospitals, with offline capabilities for unreliable infrastructure.
