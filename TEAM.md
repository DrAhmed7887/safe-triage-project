## Team Overview

We are the **Avengers** — a multidisciplinary team from the American University in Cairo (AUC), School of Business. Our capstone thesis project, **SAFE-Triage**, is a production-deployed AI-powered triage system designed specifically for Egyptian hospitals.

The system is currently live at [safe-triage-ai.web.app](https://safe-triage-ai.web.app), validated against international datasets, and submitted to the MedGemma Impact Challenge on Kaggle.

## Harvard Application

### Why This Team?

1. **Real Clinical Problem** — 86% ED staff violence rates and 32% preventable mortality in Egyptian hospitals. This isn't theoretical — our physician-developer has seen it firsthand.

2. **Production System, Not a Prototype** — SAFE-Triage is live and processing real triage decisions. This is a working clinical tool with validated metrics.

3. **Novel Contribution** — First system to handle Egyptian Arabic dialect for emergency triage, with 1,858 colloquial medical keywords and MedGemma quality assurance.

4. **Safety-First Philosophy** — "AI Extracts → Rules Decide → Humans Confirm" puts patient safety above model accuracy.

5. **Validated Performance** — 97.7% within-1 accuracy, 0.7% critical under-triage, validated across MIMIC-IV-ED, MIETIC, and Korean KTAS datasets.

6. **LMIC Focus** — Built for low- and middle-income country hospitals, with offline capabilities for unreliable infrastructure.
