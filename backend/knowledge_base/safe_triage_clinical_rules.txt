SAFE-TRIAGE CLINICAL RULES — SYSTEM-SPECIFIC DECISION LOGIC
==============================================================
Source: SAFE-Triage deterministic engine v2.0


ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLE
-----------------------
"AI Extracts → Rules Decide → Humans Confirm"

1. AI Layer: Gemini 2.5-flash extracts the chief complaint into a fixed
   symptom category (e.g., "chest_pain_cardiac", "respiratory_distress").
2. Rules Layer: Deterministic ESI v5 + NEWS2 engine assigns the ESI level
   using the category, vital signs, patient demographics, and red flags.
3. Human Layer: The treating clinician reviews and confirms or overrides
   the ESI assignment. All overrides are audited.

The AI layer can only classify — it NEVER directly assigns an ESI level.
The deterministic rules always have final authority.


SAFETY FLOOR RULES (CANNOT BE OVERRIDDEN BY AI)
-------------------------------------------------

Safety floors are hard-coded escalation rules. They can only RAISE
the ESI level, never lower it. The AI confidence score does not
affect safety floors.

1. NEWS2 ≥ 7 or any single parameter = 3 → Forces ESI 1
2. NEWS2 ≥ 5 → Forces minimum ESI 2
3. NEWS2 ≥ 3 → Forces minimum ESI 3
4. Chest pain (any presentation) → Forces minimum ESI 2
5. Stroke FAST positive (≥ 2/3 components) → Forces ESI 1 or 2
6. Neonate (0-28 days) with fever ≥ 38°C → Forces ESI 2
7. Immunocompromised patient with fever → Forces ESI 2
8. GCS ≤ 8 → Forces ESI 1
9. SBP < 70 → Forces ESI 1
10. SpO2 < 85% with respiratory distress → Forces ESI 1

These safety floors are the primary mechanism preventing under-triage.
Over-triage (assigning a higher level than necessary) is always
acceptable; under-triage (assigning a lower level) is not.


RED FLAG DEFINITIONS
---------------------

Life-threatening red flags (trigger ESI 1):
• airway_obstruction — Complete or near-complete airway blockage
• anaphylaxis — Severe allergic reaction with cardiovascular/respiratory compromise
• angioedema_airway — Angioedema threatening the airway
• severe_respiratory_distress — Respiratory failure, accessory muscle use, cyanosis
• apnea — Absence of breathing
• intubated — Patient already on mechanical ventilation
• respiratory_arrest — Cessation of breathing
• severe_asthma_attack — Life-threatening asthma (silent chest, cyanosis)
• tension_pneumothorax — Tension pneumothorax with hemodynamic compromise
• cardiac_arrest — No pulse, no breathing
• pulseless — Absence of palpable pulse
• active_massive_hemorrhage — Uncontrolled major bleeding
• hemodynamic_instability — Shock (tachycardia + hypotension + poor perfusion)
• shock — Circulatory failure (septic, hypovolemic, cardiogenic, obstructive)
• unresponsive — No response to any stimulus
• gcs_3_to_8 — Severe impairment of consciousness
• status_epilepticus — Continuous seizure > 5 minutes or repeated without recovery
• actively_seizing — Active convulsive seizure
• major_trauma — Multi-system trauma, significant mechanism of injury
• penetrating_chest_wound — Open chest wound

High-risk red flags (trigger ESI 2):
• radiation_to_arm — Chest pain radiating to left arm (ACS pattern)
• radiation_to_jaw — Chest pain radiating to jaw (ACS pattern)
• diaphoresis — Profuse sweating with pain (suggests cardiac event)
• crushing_substernal_pressure — Classic MI presentation


CONTEXT RULES (PATTERN DETECTION)
-----------------------------------

These rules detect high-risk clinical patterns that may present atypically.

1. SILENT MYOCARDIAL INFARCTION IN DIABETICS
   Triggers when ALL of:
   • Male ≥ 50 years OR any patient ≥ 65 years
   • Known diabetes (in comorbidities)
   • GI body system (nausea, vomiting, epigastric pain) OR cardiac OR heartburn
   • Vital sign abnormality: HR > 95 bpm OR RR > 20/min
   Action: Upgrade to ESI 2 with reasoning "Silent MI risk pattern in diabetic patient"

2. ATYPICAL ACUTE CORONARY SYNDROME IN WOMEN
   Triggers when ALL of:
   • Female ≥ 45 years
   • GI, cardiac, or musculoskeletal body system
   • Atypical ACS signals: jaw pain, fatigue, nausea, back pain
   Action: Upgrade to ESI 2 with reasoning "Atypical ACS pattern in female patient"

3. SEPSIS SCREENING
   Triggers when ALL of:
   • Immunocompromised, diabetic, elderly (≥ 65), or cancer patient
   • Sepsis-related body system (infectious, urinary, respiratory, GI, skin)
   • Temperature > 38.5°C
   • At least 2 of: HR > 100, RR > 22, altered mental status
   Action: Upgrade to ESI 2 with reasoning "Sepsis screening positive"


ECTOPIC PREGNANCY DETECTION
-----------------------------
Triggers when ALL of:
• Female, age 15-50 years
• Acute abdominal or back pain
• Acute onset
• At least one abnormal vital: HR ≥ 100, SBP < 100, RR ≥ 22, SpO2 < 94%
Action: Upgrade to ESI 2 with reasoning "High-risk ectopic pregnancy pattern"


SYMPTOM CATEGORY MAPPING
--------------------------

The AI layer classifies complaints into standardized categories:

ESI Level 1 categories:
cardiac_arrest, respiratory_arrest, unconscious, active_seizure,
severe_trauma, anaphylaxis, poisoning_overdose, choking_airway

ESI Level 2 categories:
chest_pain_cardiac, stroke_symptoms, respiratory_distress, severe_bleeding,
altered_mental_status, psychiatric_emergency, obstetric_emergency,
severe_pain, diabetic_emergency, allergic_reaction_moderate

ESI Level 3 categories:
abdominal_pain_moderate, moderate_trauma, headache_severe,
fever_with_symptoms, vomiting_diarrhea_dehydration,
urinary_symptoms_severe, pediatric_moderate

ESI Level 4 categories:
minor_trauma, fever_simple, mild_pain, skin_rash_stable,
minor_bleeding, cold_flu_symptoms, ear_eye_throat

ESI Level 5 categories:
chronic_refill, minor_complaint, medical_certificate


RESOURCE ESTIMATION
--------------------

Resources are estimated based on the complaint category, severity,
and clinical context. Resource modifiers include:

• Fever: Adds labs (blood cultures, CBC)
• Vomiting/dehydration: Adds IV fluids
• Age extremes (< 1 year or > 65 years): Adds monitoring, labs
• Comorbidities: Adds specialty consult
• NEWS2 ≥ 3: Adds monitoring
• High AI confidence in complex category: Adds specialty consult

Mapping:
• 0 resources → ESI 5
• 1 resource → ESI 4
• 2+ resources → ESI 3


BILINGUAL SUPPORT
------------------

SAFE-Triage supports 1,858 Arabic medical keywords including Egyptian
colloquial dialect variants. The AI layer processes complaints in both
Arabic and English. Examples:
• "ألم في الصدر" (chest pain) → chest_pain_cardiac
• "مش قادر أتنفس" (can't breathe) → respiratory_distress
• "وجع بطن شديد" (severe abdominal pain) → abdominal_pain_moderate
• "دوخة وتنميل" (dizziness and numbness) → stroke_symptoms

All educational explanations are available in both English and Egyptian
Arabic to match the clinician's preferred language.
